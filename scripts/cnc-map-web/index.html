    Jogging limits & shortcuts
    In Hold mode, Shift + arrow also starts fast XY travel. Z keeps the selected speed. Allow room to stop; firmware limits are not a clearance check.
    Arrows: XY · Page Up/Down: Z. Release Hold to cancel and decelerate. Each hold: up to 100 mm XY / 5 mm Z. Single Z steps: up to 1 mm. Z locks after the first corner.
